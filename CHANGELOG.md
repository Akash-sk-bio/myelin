# Changelog

## 3.0.0 — Myelin — 2026-11-16

### Changed
- **Renamed BioPulse → Myelin**, with a new logo: a liquid-glass pointer segmented like a myelinated axon. Settings, the original-pointer backup and the start-at-sign-in entry migrate automatically.
- **Liquid Glass pointers** (Clear and Tinted) replace the Neon and Fluid styles.
- **One-page app.** A live stage plus options column in the Apple configurator style, three premium looks (Signature, Crystal, Ember) and a customization gateway, replacing the six-page app and the 11-mood gallery.
- **Mac-like window.** Traffic-light controls, absolute black and white themes (or follow Windows), and a correct maximize that no longer covers the taskbar.

### Added
- **Live updates.** Changes reach your real pointer in about 0.5 s through a single-size quick pack, then the full pack sharpens every size.
- A dark/light preview backdrop on the stage and a "show all 17 pointers" shelf.

## 2.0.0 — 2026-11-16

### Added
- **Fluid shape**: smooth, curvy pointers with a tail-less agent-style arrow, a solid colour body, a white rim and a soft drop shadow. Available for every pointer and every colour, plus two Fluid moods (Agent, Glacier).
- **New trails**: neural constellation, ECG pulse and agent glide (a spring halo). These join the comet star with ferrocene molecules and the DNA helix.
- **New clicks**: Atom (electron orbitals) and Mitosis (a cell splits in two).
- **Extras**: double-click mitosis, a drag tether beam, and an idle "thinking" orbit.
- **New app UI** (WPF): custom title bar, sidebar navigation, Home / Moods / Cursors / Effects / Settings / About pages, toggle switches, live animated cursor previews and live effect demos in every picker.
- **Brand**: logo (PNG, SVG, multi-size ICO) and a GitHub social preview.
- **Renderer**: geometry rounding (morphological closing + opening on the distance field), soft shadows and a distance-field cache.
- **Release kit**: MIT license, CI and tag-triggered release workflows, and `--brand` / `--snap` commands for assets and screenshots.

## 1.0.0 — 2026-11-16

- First release: 17 animated Neon HUD pointers (ECG arrow, DNA-spindle loaders), 9 moods, comet + ferrocene and DNA helix trails, Synapse and Molecule clicks, tray app, hotkeys, export with `install.inf`, and a Win32 self-test.
